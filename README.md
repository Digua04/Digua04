# Chanyuan Liu

I am a security researcher interested in **industrial control system (ICS/OT) security, fuzzing, and access control**.

## Selected Research

### ICS-Sniper — NDSS 2027

**ICS-Sniper: A Targeted Blackhole Attack on Encrypted ICS Traffic**

G. Mitra, **C. Liu**, P. Dash, Y. Yao, A. Zhiyanov, A. Mehta, and K. Pattabiraman  
*Network and Distributed System Security Symposium (NDSS), 2027*

My contributions include:
- Built a **PLC-in-the-loop ICS testbed with six real Allen-Bradley PLCs** to evaluate the attack in a realistic industrial environment.
- Evaluated **eight intrusion detection methods** against ICS-Sniper attacks.
- Conducted experiments on encrypted PLC–SCADA communication and attack stealthiness.

**Artifact:**  
[Code](https://github.com/ICS-Sniper/)
[Website](https://ics-sniper.github.io/ics-sec-website/)

### ProfessorX — SACMAT 2025

**ProfessorX: Detecting Silent Vulnerabilities in Policy Engine Implementations**

B. Weintraub*, **C. Liu***, W. Enck, and C. Nita-Rotaru  
*30th ACM Symposium on Access Control Models and Technologies (SACMAT), 2025*  
\* Equal contribution

My contributions include:
- Developed **specification-guided policy mutations** that preserve the validity constraints of NGAC policies.
- Contributed to the evaluation of discrepancies across authorization policy-engine implementations.
- Worked on transpiling and validating policies across heterogeneous implementations.

[Code](https://github.com/iowaguy/professorx/tree/sacmat-2025)
